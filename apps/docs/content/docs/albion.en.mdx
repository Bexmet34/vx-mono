# Albion Online Systems

Veyronix offers the most comprehensive toolset specifically developed for Albion Online guilds and alliances. You can instantly track your members' in-game achievements and easily manage your operations.

## Killboard Integration

Automatically pulls the kills and deaths from in-game battles to your Discord server and presents them with an elegant card design.

- **Filtering:** You can filter out kills above a certain Fame or separate PVE/PVP deaths.
- **Top Lists:** Automatically ranks the top killers or top fame earners on a weekly or monthly basis and shares them in a channel you specify.

## ZvZ Party Builder

An excellent tool for gathering your team before large-scale (ZvZ) operations.
- Create an announcement by specifying the planned time and meeting location (Hideout, City, etc.).
- Participants can sign up for roles such as Tank, Healer, DPS, or Support.
- The party leader (Caller) can view the participant list and missing roles instantly on a single panel.

## Character Matching (Synchronization)

Members can link their Discord accounts with their Albion Online characters.
This makes permission and role assignments within the server fully automated. (e.g., Ensuring only guild members can view the Discord server).
