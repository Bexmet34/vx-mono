# Albion Online ZvZ and Killboard Engine

Veyronix offers a unique, real-time in-game integration for Guilds playing the Albion Online game and organizing over Discord.

Thanks to this module, you can automatically sync your in-game player roster with your Discord server and instantly reflect your guild's ZvZ (Zerg vs Zerg) performance (who killed whom, who died where) to your Discord channels in real time.

---

## 1. Guild Synchronization

When you enter your Albion Online information via the Dashboard, Veyronix communicates with the game's official API servers to audit your players' status.

### Synchronization Settings (Dashboard)
- **Game Server:** You choose which of Albion's global servers you play on (e.g., Europe, Americas, Asia). This ensures the bot connects to the correct database.
- **Guild ID / Name:** You enter the exact ID of your in-game guild (you can get this from the Albion Killboard) or its exact name.
- **How It Works:** Once this info is entered, Veyronix cross-references the names of the members registered on your server with the player names in the Albion database.
- **Benefit:** If a person leaves the guild or is kicked out, their permissions on Discord can also be automatically restricted or their name can be edited (Auto-Weeding). This prevents spies from secretly reading the guild channels.

---

## 2. Killboard (Kill/Death Notifications)

The system that reflects the enemies your players kill or the situations they die in during battles (Gank, ZvZ, Hellgate) as rich visual reports (Embed) to the Discord channels you designate.

### Web Dashboard Killboard Settings
You can assign two different notification channels from the **Killboard** tab on the Dashboard:
- **Kill Channel:** The victory channel where reports will be sent when your guild members defeat opponents (e.g., `[VX] John Killed [Enemy] Mike`). The reports include tactical data such as the weapon used, Item Power (IP), and Party info.
- **Death Channel:** The report channel where it will drop when your members die to opponents. This way, you can analyze where, to whom, and with what gear composition your players died, allowing you to manage the re-gear process after ZvZ.

<Callout type="info">
  **Real-Time Latency:** Depending on the speed at which the Albion Online API publishes data, a death experienced in-game usually drops into your Discord channel within 2 to 5 minutes. Veyronix operates on an infrastructure that keeps this ping time to a minimum.
</Callout>

## Setup Steps

To integrate the Albion module into your server:

<Steps>
<Step>
### Enter Settings from Dashboard
Log in to the [Veyronix Dashboard](https://veyronix.com.tr) and click on the **Albion Online (Killboard)** tab.
</Step>

<Step>
### Server and Guild Information
Select the location you play in (Europe, etc.) and enter your exact in-game Guild name / ID.
</Step>

<Step>
### Designate Report Channels
Select the `#kills` and `#deaths` channels you prepared on Discord from the relevant menus (Kill Channel ID / Death Channel ID).
</Step>

<Step>
### Save and Wait
After saving the settings, the system is automatically triggered in the background. As soon as your players start fighting, your logs will begin to flow!
</Step>
</Steps>
