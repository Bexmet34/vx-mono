# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## ❌ The bot is not responding to some commands, why?
This is usually a Role Hierarchy issue. For Veyronix to work properly:
1. Go to `Server Settings` > `Roles`.
2. Make sure the role named Veyronix is **ABOVE** the other roles you give to your members.
3. Ensure you have granted the `Administrator` permission to the bot.

## 🎙️ Temporary voice channels are not being deleted, what could be the problem?
The bot needs the `Manage Channels` permission to delete those channels. Also, if the channel was manually modified by you or if there was a temporary disruption caused by Discord, you can clear the empty channels manually. The bot will delete subsequent channels without any issues.

## 🛡️ Will Veyronix protect my server if it is attacked from the outside?
Veyronix is primarily an automation and community management bot. However, it does feature Anti-Spam and basic registration security measures. For large-scale attacks (Raids), we strongly recommend using Discord's own built-in "AutoMod" system alongside Veyronix.

## 💎 How can I activate Premium features?
You can purchase Premium by visiting the Dashboard at [veyronix.com.tr](https://veyronix.com.tr) and navigating to the Store tab. Once the purchase is complete, all locked advanced features in your server (Custom bot name, colored logs, etc.) will be automatically activated.
