# Community and Interaction Modules

Veyronix offers advanced gamification and communication tools not only for moderation but also to help your members spend more time on the server and build an organic bond.

In this section, we will examine two massive infrastructures managed via the Web Dashboard: the **Smart Drop System** and the **Advanced Ticket System**.

---

## 1. Smart Drop System (Events & Drops)

This system, where surprise boxes drop in chat channels and the first person to grab the box wins a prize, goes far beyond simple randomness; it has a **Chat Burst/Silence Analyzer** intelligence.

The advanced drop settings you can configure from the Web Dashboard are:

### Basic Settings and Rewards
- **System Toggle:** Completely turns the feature on and off.
- **Target Channels (Channel IDs):** You can ensure drops only fall in the channels you specify (e.g., #general-chat, #lfg).
- **Reward Type:**
  - *Coin (Points):* Gives server economy points to the winning person. (You determine the amount with `Reward Amount`).
  - *Role:* Directly gives a role you select (e.g., "Lucky of the Week" or "VIP") to the lucky person who clicks the box.

### Timing and Chance Model (Schedule Type)
Veyronix wants you to decide how the drops will fall:
1. **Random Interval:** For example, you can say "Drop randomly between a minimum of 30 minutes and a maximum of 120 minutes." The system picks a random time between these two durations.
2. **Hourly Chance:** "A 25% chance of a box dropping every hour on the hour." This way, you can keep members in the chat right on the hour (e.g., 14:00, 15:00).
3. **Exact Minutes:** "Drop only on the 15th and 45th minutes of the hours."

### Drop Chance and Cooldown
- **Chance Factor (Drop Chance):** You can set the difficulty of the box dropping as "Easy, Medium, Hard, Impossible" or directly as a custom percentage (e.g., 15%).
- **Cooldown:** To prevent boxes from dropping back-to-back, you can enforce a minimum time that must pass (e.g., 15 minutes) after a box drops.

### Smart Chat Analyzer (Only on Veyronix)
The bot can drop boxes tactically by reading how fast the chat is flowing:
- **Silence Threshold:** If no one has spoken in the chat for, say, "15 minutes," the bot can instantly throw a Drop into the middle to liven up the chat!
- **Burst Threshold:** If "more than 30 messages were sent in the last 180 seconds" (meaning the chat is on fire), the bot throws a surprise box to double the excitement.

## 2. Advanced Ticket System

The support system that allows players on your server to reach founders and moderators securely, neatly, and privately. It prevents dozens of people from harassing staff via DMs (Direct Messages).

### Setup Parameters
- **Ticket Category:** You choose under which folder/category all the private support rooms to be opened will be hosted.
- **Button Channel:** The channel where members will see the "Open Support Ticket" menu.
- **Maximum Ticket Limit:** You prevent spam/troll attacks by limiting how many tickets a member can open at the same time (e.g., Max 1).

### Category (Topic) Options
Instead of having the member open a direct ticket, you can present them with an elegant selection menu. You can add as many topic titles as you want via the dashboard.
- *Label:* Complaint Report
- *Description:* Click to report a member.
- *Value & Emoji:* 🚨 (Icon to appear on the button).

### Authorization and Naming
- **Staff Roles:** Who will see the support requests? By selecting only the Founder and Moderator roles, you prevent ordinary members from reading support rooms.
- **Ticket Name Format:** How should the name of the opened channel be?
  - `ticket-{username}` (e.g., ticket-john)
  - `{topic}-{username}` (e.g., complaint-john) you organize it with variables like these.

### 3. Ticket History (HTML Transcript)
Veyronix doesn't just delete and throw away the conversation inside when the ticket is closed.
- It backs up every word spoken, pictures, and files sent in a modern web page layout (HTML Transcript).
- By entering the **Ticket History** tab on the Web Dashboard, you can keep track of who received support on what topic in the past for months and download it to your computer at any time.

<Callout type="info">
  **Pro Tip:** Transcripts are a perfect tool for auditing your staff's attitude towards members (Quality Assurance).
</Callout>
