# Community Management and Engagement Tools

These are dedicated modules we provide to establish active communication with your users and boost server activity.

## 1. Advanced Ticket (Support) System

Creates button-based support rooms so your members can privately contact the staff.
- **Categorized Structure:** You can categorize support requests as Complaints, Suggestions, Support, etc.
- **HTML Transcript:** Every closed ticket conversation is automatically recorded and sent to your server log channel as a readable HTML (Web page) file.
- **Task Assignment:** You can assign a specific staff member to a support request.

## 2. Drop and Point System

An excellent tool to revitalize the chat.
- At certain intervals, a random "Drop (Box)" falls into the chat.
- The first member to click earns a specific amount of points/credits.
- Earned points can be used to purchase special roles or items in the Market (Store) system.

## 3. Giveaway System

A simple and fair giveaway infrastructure.
- You can ensure only members with specific roles (e.g., VIP members) join the giveaway.
- You can set flexible durations based on Seconds, Minutes, or Days.
- When the giveaway ends, the winner is automatically announced and backup winners can be drawn.
